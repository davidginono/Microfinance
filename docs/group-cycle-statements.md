# Group cycle statements

Staff cycle and organization workspaces link to /finance/groups/cycles/{id}/statement. PDF and inline print use /statement/pdf and /statement/print. Existing preparation/authority permissions remain separate from this financial report. No borrower gains staff access or organization account-view authority from being a member, signer, receiver or payer.

The owning statement service rechecks current Group View, Loan Recording View, Loan Reports View and Financial Reports View. Export additionally requires all four corresponding Export claims. Institution and first-class loan/cycle branch scope are bound on the server. No claim is assigned automatically. Authorized native loan, member-statement and receipt links retain their destination permissions; exports contain data only.

A cycle selects distinct original linked loans: individual participant loans, one shared parent, or one organization facility. Its totals never add guarantee commitments, member shares or payer allocations as new debt. Requested principal and original funded principal are distinct from current principal, interest due, future interest, supported charges and arrears. Total outstanding adds remaining principal, interest due and supported charges, excluding future interest. Arrears are a subset of the displayed debt; the report is not a settlement quote. Write-offs do not forgive contractual debt. Unfunded amounts stay separate; unavailable funding/ledger evidence makes combined posted totals unavailable.

Shared member allocations are a separate section of that parent debt, with their existing immutable component and concession sources. Missing personal attribution stays unavailable even when the parent ledger is known. Payer allocations explain immutable group receipts, including who paid and whose components benefited. Organization ordinary receipts retain the actual payer separately from the debtor. Internal top-up settlement, charge assessment, earned-interest waiver and book adjustments are non-cash activities; future-interest exclusions remain separate from cash and contractual repricing. Original/correction links and the owning financial records are retained.

Balances are current as at the server date. From/Through filter activity and payer history only. One repeatable-read transaction assembles each response, with a 30-second timeout and the existing report concurrency limiter. Fixed scoped projections replace application-side per-member query loops. Only required loan columns are materialized. Loan/member sets are bounded to 50; each of the four tables has independent 25-row server paging (loanPage, allocationPage, page, contributionPage). Pager and refresh navigation replace the complete statement snapshot, preserving the other page positions. They must rebind after each workspace transition.

PDF/print include all supported rows, with independent 5,000-row limits on activity and payer allocations; excess is rejected with a narrowing instruction. There is no CSV/Excel export, migration, ledger opening, financial mutation, historical backfill or external transport. Rendering occurs after the database read transaction. No 1,000-RPS claim follows from these correctness/query-plan checks.

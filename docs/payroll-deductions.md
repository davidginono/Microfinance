# Payroll deduction exports

Implemented scope: employer-specific loan deduction requests, registration/profile employee numbers, frozen PDF/print and employer-ready Excel schedules, employer feedback spreadsheet imports and verified remittance preparation through the existing loan receipt batch workspace. Exporting, downloading, marking a schedule as sent or importing feedback never posts a repayment. Validating and posting the prepared receipt batch updates loans, client ledgers and GL through the existing loan-owned services.

## Setup and use

1. Clients may enter **Employee number** during registration or edit it under **Profile**. It is optional for ordinary business clients and required before payroll enrollment. It preserves leading zeros and accepts up to 50 letters, digits, spaces, periods, hyphens, underscores and slashes. It is separate from the institution's Staff Number/login identifier. A self-entered number is a declaration, not verified employment.
2. An authorized staff member opens **Payroll employers** in the sidebar and records the employer name and agreement reference for the selected branch.
3. In **Deduction instructions**, choose the employer and a disbursed loan with an existing local ledger. Record the authorized monthly limit for that loan, effective dates and references to the borrower's deduction mandate and employer evidence. The employee number is read from the client's current profile, not a hidden form field. Staff must check the evidence; recording a reference does not establish legal approval.
4. In **Payroll deductions**, create a batch for this or next month and select its expected deduction date. Preview all instruction results, using the server pager. Earlier unpaid instalments are included only when explicitly selected. Confirm any exclusions before freezing.
5. Open the frozen batch to download PDF, download Excel or print it. Excel is also available in the register's selection-dependent Actions menu. The employer document combines a client's included loans into one employee deduction, with a stable deduction reference. It contains employee number/name, reference and requested TZS amount, not loan balances, authorization evidence, unrelated finances or client identifiers.
6. **Mark as sent** records the staff member's actual delivery reference. It does not transmit the file. Download history remains separate. Cancelling a sent schedule requires a recorded employer withdrawal reference and explicit confirmation. Create a linked replacement from the cancelled batch; historical rows and documents remain unchanged. Send/withdrawal actions require real supporting evidence.

## Amounts and exceptions

The proposed amount is the smaller of the instruction's per-loan monthly limit and the unpaid contractual principal plus scheduled interest on instalments due by the deduction date. Unless arrears inclusion is selected, only instalments in the payroll month are included. Paid amounts, future-interest exclusions and recorded interest waivers reduce their corresponding obligations. This reads ledger instalment counters; it does not reprice contracts or use borrower JSON estimates as posted payments. Fees/charges and future-principal collection requests are outside this export.

Amounts reflect the records available when calculated, not a reconstructed historical balance. Payments after freezing do not rewrite an employer instruction. Staff must withdraw/cancel and replace an obsolete schedule before payroll processing. The per-loan limits do not establish a salary-wide affordability or statutory deduction ceiling; the institution must retain the applicable authorized limits and evidence for each arrangement.

Expired/not-yet-effective authorizations, changed/missing employee numbers, inactive clients, unsupported loans, overlapping active batches and no unpaid instalments are explicit exclusions. Changing an employee number requires stopping and replacing its old instruction. A loan cannot have two active instructions; an employer employee number cannot map to different clients. Stopping an instruction affects future batches, not a schedule already frozen.

## Security, storage and limits

- Routes start at `/finance/payroll`. Registration/profile retain session authentication and CSRF. Profile updates change only the authenticated active client's employee number, without overwriting concurrent access changes.
- Explicitly assign `PAYROLL_DEDUCTIONS_VIEW`, `PAYROLL_DEDUCTIONS_CREATE`, `PAYROLL_DEDUCTIONS_UPDATE` and `PAYROLL_DEDUCTIONS_EXPORT` through the existing access matrix. None is granted automatically. Current claims, active staff/institution/branch and exact tenant/branch scope are checked again in the service, including PDF/print and Excel downloads.
- V81 adds the optional member field, employer/instruction records, linked instruction stops, frozen batch headers/lines, permanent batch events and retained PDF artifacts. V81.1 indexes loan overlap lookups. Financial-history triggers protect these records; deferred checks require complete frozen rows and totals. No existing loan opening, posting or approval history is changed.
- Registers and details use 25-row server pages; choices use 25 plus lookahead. Calculation/export is capped at 1,000 active instructions per employer. Larger employers need a separately implemented partitioned/batched export. No throughput claim is made.
- Generation rejects stale previews, duplicate active employer/month batches and changed retries. Request and employer locks serialize competing commands. Snapshots include source identity, dates, amounts, exceptions, actor and a SHA-256 fingerprint. Each language's first PDF is retained; subsequent PDF/print downloads return the same bytes and checksum and append a download event.
- The 2026-10-21 user instruction authorizes employer-ready Excel deduction schedules in addition to PDF/print for this workspace only. It does not enable Excel/CSV for vouchers or other operational reports. Excel feedback retains its separate input contract. No employer API, automated dispatch, salary product, salary evidence verification or automatic bank-channel reconciliation is introduced. Existing voucher approval settings and specialized repayment safeguards remain with their owning services.

## Employer-ready spreadsheet export

Choose **Download Excel** for a frozen, sent or cancelled batch (`GET /finance/payroll/{id}/xlsx`). The authenticated staff member must currently hold both VIEW and EXPORT payroll claims in the same institution and selected branch; request parameters cannot override scope. No claim, role bundle, database schema or business rule is changed by this increment.

The `.xlsx` contains one sheet with frozen institution/branch/employer names, batch reference, payroll month, expected deduction date, calculation cutoff and current batch state. Cancelled schedules carry an explicit historical-copy warning; replacements identify their prior batch. The employee table uses the PDF's grouping: one included employee per row, employee number, employee name, stable deduction reference and requested TZS amount. Excluded/zero rows and internal client/loan IDs, loan balances, source evidence, audit actors and snapshot hashes are omitted. Totals and employee counts describe the entire frozen schedule, regardless of the details/register page or filters.

Employee numbers and references remain text, preserving leading zeros. All text is written as string cells, including text beginning with formula characters; no formulas, macros, external links or actual-deduction/payment fields are generated. Ordinary TZS values use numeric cells with two decimal places. Amounts beyond Excel's 15-digit precision are stored as exact decimal text, following the statement export pattern, rather than rounded. Column widths, wrapped names, a frozen header, employee filters and repeated A4 print headings support employer use.

The existing 1,000-instruction bound and shared export concurrency limiter apply. Generation occurs outside database transactions, from stored batch headers/lines only; it does not refresh products, read live repayment amounts or recalculate instructions. Subsequent profile changes, repayments and stopped instructions do not alter exported deductions. Spreadsheet files are regenerated from the immutable snapshot; unlike retained PDFs, byte-identical XLSX artifacts are not stored or promised. Current lifecycle labels can change. Each completed generation/download request appends a permanent `DOWNLOAD` event with `XLSX`, language and SHA-256 checksum. PDF retention remains separate. Responses are attachments with the XLSX media type, `Cache-Control: no-store`, `nosniff` and `X-Document-SHA256`.

This workbook is the deduction request schedule, not the seven-column feedback input template below. It does not prefill successful deductions or evidence. Downloading does not mark a schedule sent, import feedback, prepare receipts, post repayments, change balances or create a journal. Employers still report actual results using the existing feedback contract; verified remittance preparation and receipt posting remain separate authorized actions.

## Employer feedback and remittance

Open a sent batch and choose **Deduction feedback → Import feedback**. The authenticated institution/branch and selected frozen batch determine the scope. Import one `.xlsx` worksheet (maximum 2 MB, 1,000 result rows) with these seven exact headings, in order:

| Column | Required value |
| --- | --- |
| Batch reference | Full `PAY-...` reference on the sent schedule, stored as text |
| Employee number | Exact frozen employee number, stored as text to preserve leading zeros |
| Deduction reference | Employee's deduction reference on the employer PDF, stored as text |
| Status | `SUCCESS` or `FAILED` |
| Deducted TZS | Actual positive amount for SUCCESS, zero for FAILED; at most two decimal places |
| Deduction date | Text `YYYY-MM-DD`, required for SUCCESS, within the payroll month and no later than today |
| Employer reference | Employer's result/evidence reference, maximum 250 characters |

Upload evidence is mandatory. All rows are checked before anything is retained; an invalid row rejects the entire file and identifies its spreadsheet row. Unknown/excluded employees, other batches, mismatched numbers/references, excess amounts, duplicate employees/references, numeric identifiers, formulas, external links, macros, extra sheets and oversized/decompression-heavy files are rejected. Absent employees remain unreported; zero/failed deductions never become receipts. Both partial positive deductions and failed results are retained with original bytes, checksum, semantic fingerprint, matched identity, requested amount and actor. A metadata-only or reordered exact file retry returns the original import. Each employee accepts one final positive result for this payroll batch; a later successful result following a failure is allowed, while another positive result is rejected, even after reversal. Corrections require retained evidence and the existing receipt correction workflow rather than reimporting money.

Deducted salary is distinct from money received by the lender. On the feedback details page, an accountant verifies the receiving account, actual date received, bank/payment reference, evidence and amount. The amount must equal this file's successful deductions; the date cannot precede them. Each payment reference/account/branch can be prepared only once. Resolve combined remittances, missing funds, excess funds or other differences before preparation; this increment does not allocate a single bank credit across several feedback files or invent unapplied credit.

**Prepare receipts** atomically creates a sealed draft receipt batch: one receipt per successful employee, with only that employee's included frozen loans. A partial deduction is allocated in frozen loan-row order, capped at each loan's requested amount. The existing receipt batch shows the actual loan splits before posting. Source amounts, clients, loan selections and remittance details cannot be edited/deleted or supplemented after preparation. One receipt supports at most 50 loans. Preparation and exact retries create no money entries; changed retries fail.

The preparing accountant opens the existing receipt batch, validates it and posts it. Its durable worker rechecks current accounting and loan permissions, repayment policy, product account bindings, loan balances, periods and scope. Unsupported loans, missing verified ledger openings/account configuration, outstanding-charge restrictions of that workspace, changed balances or other validation failures remain blocked and visible; they are never silently treated as paid. Each employee receipt posts atomically through `LoanReceiptService` and `LoanRecordingService`, including repayment allocations, GL, audit and balance/status synchronization. Other employees may complete while a row fails; the batch retains those outcomes and supports the existing validation/retry flow. No extra payroll journal is created. Feedback details show pending/blocked/posted states and link to authorized receipts. Independent linked reversals use the normal receipt workflow and appear as Reversed in feedback.

Explicitly assign `PAYROLL_DEDUCTIONS_IMPORT` for uploads and `PAYROLL_DEDUCTIONS_POST` for verified remittance preparation, in addition to VIEW. Preparation also requires existing receipt/loan view/create/post claims and the accountant role; no permissions are assigned automatically. Receipt posting/correction/export retains its existing separate claims. A payroll batch with feedback cannot be cancelled or replaced; historical requests/results remain intact. V82 adds immutable feedback/remittance/link tables, scoped indexes, complete-row/total checks and sealed receipt-source protections. There is no change to existing posted loan history or approval settings.

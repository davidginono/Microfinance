# Group coverage cases

Coverage uses an owning service and append-only PostgreSQL history, localized staff/client workspaces, exact posted-receipt selection, case PDF/print and scoped statement traces. The [completion audit](group-lending-completion-audit.md) records the current supported boundaries and verification; the backend evidence below describes the original increment.

A case refers to an accepted directed reciprocal guarantee, an accepted separate personal guarantee, or a signed shared allocation. JOINT cases retain the recorded joint-and-several ceiling. PROPORTIONAL shared allocations produce VOLUNTARY requests; they do not become guarantees for other participants. An organization remains the debtor on its own account. No case, acknowledgement, dispute or promise creates another receivable, posts cash or releases the original guarantee/debt.

The institution must explicitly record the approved collection-policy reference for the branch/product, with notice, case and promise limits, before calls can be recorded. Revisions retain their maker/time and predecessor; existing cases retain their original policy. No policy is seeded or approved by the application. Calls require a disbursed scoped loan, its real repayment ledger, a currently identifiable named person, a verified Kiswahili/bilingual served-notice PDF and separate borrower/person service records. Both service dates must relate to the actual missed due date and precede the call by the configured notice period (at least fourteen days). Applying that minimum to both recipients is a conservative product gate; regulation 56's express fourteen-day requirement concerns the borrower. Staff must verify applicability and approved content independently; a PDF upload is not proof of lawful service.

The requested amount is limited to currently unpaid principal and interest on instalments already overdue when the call is recorded, the unreserved shortfall and the retained source ceiling. Fees without supported due-date evidence are excluded from the call calculation. Money subsequently received retains all its exact posted principal/interest/charge components. Original due dates remain frozen: curing an old shortfall does not revive the same case when another instalment falls due. Closing/cancelling/expiry affects the operational request only; accepted guarantee evidence and debt remain intact. Missing ledger history is unavailable, never zero.

Only the named client session can acknowledge, dispute or promise, with current explicit permissions, confirmation, sequence and idempotency checks. Staff cannot impersonate these responses. Promises fit the remaining original shortfall and the frozen expiry/promise limits. Staff may record reasoned closure/cancellation. Every event remains immutable and auditable. No notices, collection messages or demands are sent by these commands.

Received coverage links a complete existing posted payer source; no user-entered second cash amount is accepted. Group receipts require the exact identified contribution row, loan and payer. Other supported receipts retain their actual payer text and require staff verification evidence; a matching name never proves identity automatically. Each uncorrected source can be linked only once. Actual whole receipts may exceed the requested instalment shortfall, but cannot exceed the retained signed ceiling; ordinary receipt limits/waterfalls remain with the financial owning services. Late receipt evidence may be linked after case expiry without reopening a call.

Financial receipt reversals automatically remove their coverage credit while preserving the original link and correcting reference. Attribution mistakes use a separate append-only correction by another explicitly authorized staff member. Such corrections never reverse cash, alter the ledger or replace the original debtor. Financial corrections continue through their existing owning receipt service. Private payer-verification text is omitted from client detail views.

New Coverage View/Create/Update/Configure/Link/Reverse/Export and My Coverage View/Respond/Export claims require explicit assignment. Staff additionally need Group View and Loan Recording View. Client actions check current active client/institution/branch status and both session/current effective claims. Scope comes from the server; loan branch is first-class. Page reads return at most 25 rows plus one continuation marker. PDF parsing occurs before the short write transaction under two-upload admission. Mutations lock the original loan before the case, policy version changes serialize by branch/product, and rendering is outside these transactions.

# Organization account viewing

A verified organization authority revision may explicitly grant a named person View organization account. Existing authority rows default to false. Applying, signing, receiving funds, paying a receipt or group membership does not grant viewing. A person may have viewing power alone within an otherwise complete borrowing mandate.

The client Group loans workspace lists authorized organization accounts separately from signing and payout requests. The account statement, PDF and inline print use /app/group-loans/{cycle}/organization-account. No representative becomes a personal borrower, gains staff privileges or receives a link to staff financial records.

Each request validates the current active client, institution, branch and MEMBER_LOANS_VIEW in both the session and current effective claims. PDF/print additionally require current LOAN_DOCUMENTS_VIEW and LOAN_DOCUMENTS_EXPORT. The latest authority for the organization must explicitly name the viewer, be within its inclusive validity dates, unrevoked, and belong to the same active organization/tenant/branch as the original cycle and loan. Replacing an authority grants only the replacement powers. Revoking or expiring it never revives an older grant. Current authority may authorize viewing an older loan without rewriting the frozen origination mandate, signatures, pricing or payout evidence. Branch moves do not widen access.

The read service reuses the scoped cycle statement financial projections and renderer. One company loan supplies principal, due interest, future interest, supported charges, arrears, outstanding and next due; member shares are absent. Actual payer and original/correction references remain visible as company cash history. Internal settlement, concessions and book adjustments remain non-cash. Balances are current; activity dates do not certify historical balances. Missing opening evidence stays unavailable. There are no member debts, automatic guarantees, new money postings, claim assignments or financial backfills.

Each screen is one read-only repeatable-read transaction with a 30-second timeout and existing report admission limit. Activity is paged at 25; complete export rejects more than 5,000 rows. Queries and client discovery are bounded and have no application query-per-person loop. The partial viewer index leads with client_id and authority_id for explicit grants; existing authority history/revocation/facility indexes serve scope and latest-revision checks. There is no new executor or external work under the read transaction. PDF rendering follows the transaction.

V123 adds the false-default power and permits an additional view-only person while preserving existing immutable-history, identity scope and deferred complete-mandate guards. Existing role/default claims and all financial history remain unchanged. This increment does not establish approved disclosure content, coverage-case handling, required personal guarantees or measured throughput.

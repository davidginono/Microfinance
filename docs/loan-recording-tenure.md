# Loan tenure and recording

New product settings and loan forms capture a tenure unit (days, months or years) and a positive whole number. Existing month contracts are retained. The compatibility `tenor_months` field contains the ceiling month equivalent; new quotes retain the exact unit/value in their server-generated snapshot and use that snapshot for pricing and disbursement.

Month and year quotes preserve the existing periodic calculation. Day quotes use a fixed 365-day annual basis, with up to `ceil(days * 12 / 365)` monthly or `ceil(days * 52 / 365)` weekly payments. Reducing-balance day quotes distribute the annual rate over those periods proportionally to the exact duration. Flat annual interest uses the same duration basis. Day-loan monthly affordability scales the largest payment by the payment count divided by the exact month-equivalent duration, rounded upward. A short day loan cannot use a full month of declared cash flow for each payment. These are application conventions, not a regulatory or institutional-policy approval. They do not change an existing disbursed contract.

Day schedules mature exactly the entered number of days after disbursement. The first due date must allow all scheduled payments before that maturity; a one-payment day loan is due on its maturity date. Month/year dates retain the existing first-date-anchored conventions. The first due date is chosen at disbursement, and assessed amounts, rate and payment count remain protected.

Accountant recording starts with Configured loan product or Customised loan. Configured loans retain product availability, amount/tenure limits and existing fee restrictions. Customised recording supports a percentage of principal for the whole loan, an annual flat percentage, or an explicitly entered total interest amount. The recorder chooses the percentage basis. Recording creates a loan application and sends it to the selected product's staff review stages. It does not post money or silently add fees. Customised recording requires the institution admin's customised workflow configuration.

Customised reducing-balance loans support annual or whole-loan percentages. For **For the whole loan**, the entered rate is divided by the actual scheduled payment count, then charged on the remaining principal using the shared decimal annuity calculator. For example, 12% over six monthly payments uses 2% per payment; total interest is below 12% of the original principal. Days, months, years and weekly schedules use their exact existing payment counts. The selected basis and original percentage remain in the server-generated financial snapshot and are reused for previews and disbursement; annual, configured-product and unversioned legacy calculations retain their conventions. Reducing balance still requires percentage entry, explicit tenure and the existing rate/payment bounds. This software behavior does not establish an approved institutional pricing policy.

Quote interest and disbursement amounts are serialized as decimal strings so browser parsing cannot lose cents. Interest is calculated through the shared decimal calculator and displayed beside Loan amount (Principal). The spinner reflects an actual cancellable quote request. A configured quote before tenure entry identifies the product's minimum tenure used for the preview; the entered tenure is required to save. A quote is not verification of creditworthiness or a posting.

Repayment schedules use the same column order in recording, borrower/staff views, repayment pages and PDF/print output: instalment number, due date, beginning balance, scheduled payment, principal, interest and ending balance. Estimates show Week 1/Week 2 or Month 1/Month 2 in the selected language, according to repayment frequency; actual schedules retain their stored due dates. A tenure in days does not introduce a daily repayment frequency. Beginning balance includes all remaining scheduled principal and interest; ending balance subtracts that row's principal and interest. For example, a TZS 10,000 loan with TZS 183.45 scheduled interest starts at TZS 10,183.45; a first payment of TZS 4,969.68 principal plus TZS 122.05 interest leaves TZS 5,091.72. These are contractual schedule balances, not today's settlement amount. Scheduled payment is the row's principal plus interest, displayed between beginning balance and principal. Payment totals remain in the quote data; payment history and paid-principal/interest columns remain available on the repayment page.

The balance projection uses decimal calculator totals or stored principal and interest components without rewriting stored schedules or payment records. Paginated ledger schedules sum contractual principal and interest from the page's first instalment onward with one loan-scoped aggregate after access authorization; existing 25-row pages remain. Partial payments do not rewrite contractual schedule balances. Legacy schedules with insufficient component data show unavailable balances rather than invented zeros. Stored partial schedules retain their existing contractual outstanding values, while complete schedules reconcile the displayed balances to zero.

Supporting documents use the existing drag/drop uploader and protected storage. Recording/disbursement accept up to ten PDF, PNG or JPEG files, each at most 25 MB, with the existing default 25 MB total request limit; content signatures and extensions are checked. Files participate in the request fingerprint. Failed transactional storage is cleaned up by the existing storage service. Downloads require the recording view claim and the record's institution/branch scope. Historical evidence references remain retained.

Migration V60 adds explicit product tenure limits, permits an unset first due date before disbursement, and adds accounting-period loan numbering. New Loan IDs start at 1 within each institution's accounting period, allocated atomically in the disbursement transaction. A rollback rolls back the number allocation. Uniqueness includes the period; legacy identifiers and their uniqueness remain retained. The loan UUID remains the record identity, since numeric IDs can repeat across periods. The number and period become immutable after assignment.

Staff Disburse retains the existing permission, scope, proof, OTP, lending approval and specialised accounting-source safeguards. Its transaction creates the disbursement/schedule/ledger records and sets Disbursed; the successful action opens the existing Disbursed Loans table, which reads the committed state. For staff-recorded applications, the teller review also requires the money account and loan principal control account. The same transaction posts the balanced journal and disbursement ticket, opens the loan ledger, assigns the period Loan ID, records the teller action and publishes the disbursed event. The standalone finance disbursement creation page redirects to the staff queue; its old POST endpoint is retired. Ordinary specialised loan disbursement safeguards remain. These paths do not create a second journal or fabricated approval evidence.

## Recorded application staff workflow ? 14 October 2026

The entry page is now **Record Loan Application**. Configured products keep their configured review roles and ordering. Institution admins configure **Customised loan workflow** in Settings & Controls: review roles/order, named board and credit committee reviewers, allowed tellers and the proof requirement. Existing single-chairperson and committee voting rules are reused. At least one eligible review stage and one eligible teller are required. Manager/accountant review eligibility remains branch-scoped; configured chairperson/board/committee assignments follow the existing product rules.

One workflow configuration is stored per institution, separate from priced products and excluded from borrower/product catalog lookups. It supplies routing, not the custom application's principal or interest. Application creation freezes the workflow, named reviewers, named tellers and proof requirement. Changing settings later does not reprice or reassign a submitted application. A selected teller must still have current disbursement permission and active access in the application's branch when posting. Legacy recorded applications have a **Send for review** action that captures routing without changing their financial snapshot.

The teller's record-specific review contains money/principal account choices, first instalment due date, reference, notes and drag/drop documents alongside the existing OTP/proof controls. Account lookups remain scoped and bounded. The recorded amount is read-only and authoritative on the server. A review cannot silently change recorded monetary terms. Success opens the Disbursed Loans table; the finance disbursement register retains the same posted ticket.

Migration **V61** adds the custom workflow marker and its per-institution unique index. Recorded applications can transition only through their frozen approval sequence or the matching stage rejection. A deferred database guard rejects a Disbursed status without its posted disbursement ticket and journal. Idempotency, per-loan locking, closed-period protection, upload checks, audit, numbering and repayment/reversal safeguards remain in place. Existing funded loans are not rerouted.

## Organization borrowing-authority records

- Scope: the group record now has a Borrowing authority workspace for named people who may apply, sign loan agreements or authorize payout. A person need not be a group member; an office title or membership does not supply these powers. Recording authority creates neither a loan nor a personal guarantee. - Evidence and lifecycle: V101 stores the group identity, validity dates, principal limit, minimum distinct signing count, checked legal-capacity/resolution/signature references, verified payout details and the recorder identity. Source references represent the authorized staff member's checks, not automated document/signature validation or actual acceptance of a future loan agreement. The simple signing-count rule must match the institution's approved resolution; richer mandatory-person rules require explicit support before use.
- Versions and corrections: a new immutable version references the prior version and requires renewed verification. The request key is actor/payload bound.

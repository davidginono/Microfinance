Chromium verified all three disclosure pages plus the configuration page in English and Kiswahili at 360, 768, 1366 and 1920 pixels. Native individual acceptance used an actual OTP delivered to local Mailpit; the recorded language remained visible. Organization/shared downloads retained the exact supplied annual cost and complete original PDF. Invalid configuration submission retained the entered method reference and six-decimal ceiling. No browser page exceptions or page overflow occurred. Screenshots, exact commands and detailed evidence are outside the repository in `/workspace/scratch/group-work`; the delivery log records the increments.

Performance capacity remains unproven: there was no load, spike or soak benchmark. Small synthetic query-plan observations are not 1,000-RPS evidence. Institution-specific regulatory classification, contract wording and the supplied annual-cost/dated-fee methodology require the institution's approved decisions and configuration. The application retains contract-owned balances and group context without inventing additional receivables or silently changing legal liability.

Phase 6 provider confirmations, unmatched/excess funds, refunds, seasonal schedules and new fees remain separate approved designs. They are disabled rather than inferred from this implementation. The supported channel is confirmed manual money that can be attributed exactly to existing obligations.

Cleanup completed: eight temporary synthetic authentication profiles, exact settings/claims, payment profile and local mail configuration were restored and verified. Temporary credentials/checkpoints were removed; the owned QA runtime stopped. Permanent synthetic consent/coverage/correction evidence remains intact.

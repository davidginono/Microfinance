# Personal guarantees for typed group loans

Organization and shared-parent applications retain their product's required guarantor count. A positive count starts the recorded loan at Awaiting Guarantors. Its borrowing/organization signatures and payout instructions remain separate evidence. A zero count retains the existing product review path. Once distinct required guarantors personally accept, an authorized staff command starts the recorded product's frozen review path; it never substitutes guarantee consent for manager, committee, board, accountant or lender execution. Database guards prevent bypass into review/funding. All existing individual origination and reciprocal-cohort flows remain separate.

Staff open Personal guarantees from the cycle or organization workspace, select an active scoped client, verify total guarantee capacity, set an explicit offer acceptance deadline and retain a checked institution-approved PDF with language, contact and complaint details. The signed ceiling is the whole frozen principal, contractual interest and supported charges, subject to the recorded group policy cap. A shared joint obligor cannot guarantee the same debt separately. Organization signing/receiving/view powers do not establish personal consent. A named organization representative may separately offer a personal guarantee if independently assessed and personally consenting. No membership, fake personal debtor, savings or shares prerequisite is created.

Each offer retains the original borrower/financial snapshot, guarantor identity, cap, capacity evidence, exact PDF bytes/checksum, packet hash, maker and date. It cannot be overwritten or deleted. Same-key identical bytes/payload retry once; changed content is rejected. A duplicate live offer is recoverably rejected. Pending offers reserve capacity until their stated deadline, refusal, scoped staff cancellation or underlying loan closure. Accepted promises remain after the offer deadline and preserve history when their loan is paid, independently corrected, written off or restored. A write-off is not forgiveness. A borrower/member exit never releases the promise. A paid debt no longer consumes active capacity; a linked payment correction restores its original commitment.

The shared commitment projection includes pending/accepted personal promises alongside ordinary and reciprocal guarantees, deduplicated per person/loan. Capacity admission locks the same scoped client key across these paths. Unknown amounts and unresolved cross-branch obligations fail the required assessment; missing history is never a clean record. Current active/pending debt, branch default/count rules, verified budget and group policy limits are checked. Concurrent offers cannot overbook one budget. Existing personal budgets also protect ordinary approvals and reciprocal group promises; malformed historical terms remain unknown and retain existing behavior when no personal budget applies. Recorded budgets are conservative: the smallest still-active verified personal budget applies; increasing that budget while old promises remain requires a separately governed reassessment capability.

The client sidebar's Group guarantees registry contains only that authenticated person's offers, with 25-row server paging. Staff have a separate scoped 25-row history. Reads/commands require current active institution/branch/client or staff identity and both session/current effective claims. Staff preparation uses Group Update plus Loan Recording Create; reads use Group View plus Loan Recording View; starting review also needs Group Submit. Client reads use Guarantor Requests View; personal acceptance uses Approve, refusal uses Reject. Source downloads and signed exports additionally require Loan Documents View/Export. No permission is assigned automatically. CSRF remains on every mutation and document-open POST. Borrower/guarantor inputs cannot bind identity scope, ceiling, status, signature or reviewer evidence.

Acceptance requires the unchanged packet, current registered personal signature, preparation of the authorized full terms download and the configured delivered confirmation code. Code attempts are bounded, failed attempts persist, consumed challenges cannot authorize another packet/person, and transport runs outside transactions. Refusal and cancellation retain their own reason/actor/date. Accepted promises cannot be cancelled through the offer command. A nominee who becomes inactive or moves branch can still have the unaccepted offer cancelled by staff in the original authorized scope. Commands serialize on the source loan before checking current decisions, avoiding duplicate acceptance and races with withdrawal/review.

Accepted guarantees support signed PDF and inline print evidence, containing the institution/address, actual typed borrower, current readable loan number, frozen ceiling/rate/components, signature/date, current loan state and original funded date when recorded. Undisbursed loans stay explicitly unfunded; no application or expected date is fabricated as a grant date. The exact checked source PDF follows the disclosure. Both English and Kiswahili cover labels are supported. The source document retains its approved language; UI translation never translates or approves the legal source. Evidence reads are repeatable-read and bounded to 30 seconds; parsing happens before the short offer transaction, rendering after the read transaction, under bounded upload/export admission. No new executor or external call runs under a money/consent lock. Operational documents remain PDF/print only.

V124 adds offer/decision/document/challenge history, commitment and typed review guards. V125 serializes competing capacity paths and rechecks accepted evidence; V126 preserves malformed legacy-term semantics and scoped cancellation after nominee changes. V127 freezes the required guarantor count and limits continuation to the recorded first review stage. Applied files are never rewritten. These migrations do not backfill approvals, rewrite financial history or grant claims.

# Accounting PDF and print formats

Updated 2026-10-11 from the supplied receipt voucher, payment voucher, trial balance, income statement and financial-position screenshots. The images are layout references, not accounting data or workflow instructions.

- Receipt, payment and journal vouchers use monochrome A4 stationery: institution/branch identity, voucher number and actual posting state, effective date/reference, payer/payee, the recorded money account, repeated transactions with ledger references, TZS totals, amount in words, narration and the actual posting identity/time. Short vouchers retain writing space; long vouchers continue with repeated headings and page numbers. Payment/journal debit and credit totals both equal the posted transaction total; the amount is not misleadingly presented as their zero net difference.
- No approval/checker boxes, invented signatures, sample company contacts, unrecorded payment channels or additional charges are added. The core voucher snapshot has no company/contact/logo or cheque fields beyond institution/branch names, party and references. These absent data are not fabricated. Existing final statement captures can include their institution-owned logo.
- Trial balance is one landscape table: account, description, brought-forward balance, debits, credits, net change and ending balance. Credit movements remain positive in the credit column; negative signed balances/net changes use parentheses. The full authorized export totals and gross opening/closing debit-credit totals remain present. Unknown openings/closings remain unavailable, never zero.
- Financial position and income statements use their approved titles, ordered headings/groups, a Notes column linked to numbered notes, right-aligned TZS amounts, and underlined subtotal/required-total rows. Comparison columns and ratio precision remain where the frozen definition requires them. Frozen statements contain approved aggregate line labels, not individual account-code snapshots: the exporter does not invent account codes or query current accounts to reinterpret a historical result. Required notes, coverage and source/cutoff/version/checksum evidence remain in the report appendix.
- The screenshot Details columns are omitted. The income example's incorrect Balance Sheet heading is not copied. PDF and print are the only outputs. Voucher/trial Print uses the same PDF renderer and authorized data as Download, not a separate HTML rendering; retained-statement Print serves the exact stored bytes. The obsolete voucher HTML print page/handler were removed; they remain recoverable in Git history.
- New statement captures use layout version 2, so they have a different layout checksum/deduplication identity from version 1. Existing output artifacts are retained and downloaded unchanged; Print likewise uses their retained bytes. Version 1 keeps its original renderer and numeric presentation. To obtain the new format for an existing finalized result, capture another output through the existing output form.

## Boundaries and verification

### Receipt/payment stationery (2026-10-12)

Receipt and payment outputs follow the third supplied screenshot: a three-column institution/logo/document header, payer/payee details beside payment details, payment-method boxes, a pale grey transaction-table heading, open writing rows, amount in words beside totals, narration and a four-column posting area. Institution-owned logos are loaded locally after document authorization; when absent the logo space remains empty. Unknown contact/cheque fields remain blank. Only a recorded channel is selected, and no extra charges are invented. Receipt/payment currency is TZS. Actual account snapshots and the posted amount remain present, with a continued table for long transactions.

The reference's approval/checker/signature evidence is not accounting data. The footer retains actual posting identity/time, status and linked corrections rather than introducing approval stages. Payment debit and credit totals each equal the posted total; the total payment is not their zero difference. Journal stationery and financial-report formats are unchanged.

Core vouchers, recorded-loan disbursements/repayments/reversals and ledger repayment receipts share `VoucherPdfDocument`. Their Print and Download actions serve the same PDF layout with inline/attachment disposition respectively. Ledger receipt exports retain their existing client-ownership and institution/branch read guard, then check the loan and client scope before reading printable identity. The loan's first-class branch supplies branch identity. No monetary mutation, database migration or new accounting workflow is included.

Verification for this stationery increment: 47 focused PDF, MVC, statement and repayment tests passed with no failures/errors/skips. English/Kiswahili, short postings, large amounts, 50 transactions, long identity/description fields and scope denials were covered. Source/PDF comparisons and final rendered pages are recorded in the current section of `design-qa.md`. Live browser verification was unavailable; this is not a full-suite, database-integration or load-test claim.

Formatting consumes existing bounded, authorized typed results. No database migration, account mapping, posting calculation, policy gate, approval workflow, financial mutation or automatic institution activation is introduced. Voucher Print calls the same resource export guard as PDF. Trial Print uses the existing scoped export. Statement Print uses the protected retained-artifact download, including current authorization, source/layout integrity and checksum checks. Existing generation admission limits remain; no threads or external logo URLs are introduced. This is not a throughput benchmark or completion of the accounting/report-builder backlog.

Preview PDFs generated by regression tests are synthetic and remain under ignored `target/`. Tests cover ordinary multi-transaction vouchers, very large amounts and exact bilingual amount-in-words, 50-row continuations, long descriptions, landscape trial totals/unavailable openings, grouped statements/comparisons/notes, 9,000 required evidence notes, PDF-only output, print/download headers, claims and resource export guards.

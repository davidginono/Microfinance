# Loan product principal and interest accounts

Implemented increment: 19 October 2026. Loan product editing, including an existing customised-loan configuration, has **Loan principal account** and **Loan interest account** selectors. New products use the automatic creation described below. Editing these links does not post money or create accounts.

2026-10-21: both account selectors, including selected-value hydration, exclude heading/group and control accounts. New assignments are validated as posting accounts on the server. Frozen loan bindings and historical journals remain unchanged.

## Automatic creation for new products (21 October 2026)

Creating a new product now generates its own principal and interest posting-account versions in the same transaction. The create form no longer asks for existing accounts. Product-creation permission delegates only these two definitions and does not grant chart-management or approval claims. Principal defaults to Asset / Debit / Posting / Other, and interest to Income / Credit / Posting / Income, under the effective Assets and Income main groups. Account codes derive from the permanent product ID, so name/code edits cannot duplicate accounts or redirect historical loans.

The institution's account-creation approval setting applies. Automatic mode approves and links both accounts immediately. Independent mode retains both submitted versions, leaves the product unlinked until both accounts are effective, and uses the existing independent approval/rejection/revision workflow. No loan may fund against unapproved accounts. Main-group setup retains the existing chart bootstrap rules; unavailable/inactive parents or any provisioning/audit failure roll back the complete product command. Existing products are not backfilled, and editing an existing product retains authorized posting-account selection.

V85 records the product, original creation versions, institution/branch and reserved account IDs while approval is pending. Account IDs are not foreign keys to effective accounts because pending accounts do not exist there yet. Scoped version references and immutable evidence guard the association. Activation of the second approved account links the pair atomically and records a system audit event; it does not overwrite a separately assigned pair. The institution setup lock, deterministic request keys and unique account/provision identities protect retries and concurrent approval. Rejected proposals can be revised against the same reserved account IDs. Generated accounts are excluded from ordinary voucher lookups and templates, and a deferred journal guard blocks ordinary/manual posting to them. Loan-owned posting, allocation and linked-correction safeguards remain authoritative. No journal, balance, repayment, worker, permission grant or historical binding is changed.

## Setup and use

- Product configuration uses the existing product create/update claims and selected institution. Both selectors search the same active posting accounts by code/name, without principal/interest account-type, normal-balance or purpose presets. Lookups remain paged (25 choices plus lookahead), including exact selected-account hydration, with no institution-wide account loading. Saving either mapping accepts only an active `POSTING` account from that institution; heading groups and control accounts cannot be newly assigned. New mappings must contain both accounts or neither. Principal and interest may point to the same posting account; the subledger still tracks the two amounts separately, while the general-ledger account total combines them. Account selection and reporting classification remain institution decisions.
- New standard products continue to receive generated principal and interest posting accounts at creation. The Loan Product editor and Customized Loan Products configuration can additionally assign an active posting-account pair; an explicitly selected pair replaces the generated pair for later disbursements. The Customized Loan Products selectors are available from the first configuration save. Leaving them blank on that first save preserves the generated accounts.
- Existing products can retain incomplete mappings, but cannot fund a directly recorded loan until both accounts are available. Disbursement resolves both links from the product and retains an immutable, institution/branch-scoped loan account binding in the same transaction as the disbursement. The principal debit uses that binding. Subsequent product edits apply to subsequent disbursements.
- Before collecting an existing directly recorded, disbursed loan without a binding, an authorized recorder opens its loan details and selects **Link product accounts**. The product's principal account must equal the original disbursement account. A mismatch blocks linking and needs a separate accounting correction. Linking retains the product's configured interest account for subsequent receipts; earlier payments, journals and reversals are unchanged. Repeating a completed link is harmless.
- Receipt and batch recorders choose the client and Loan ID. Loan choices include the product name. They do not choose the product's GL accounts. Each repayment resolves its own immutable principal/interest mapping; a multi-loan receipt or batch can therefore credit different product accounts. The receiving cash/bank/mobile-money account remains an input.
- Interest-first allocation is built in; the Repayment settings workspace was removed on 2026-10-20. No policy setup is required for new receipts or batches. Existing policy snapshots remain intact. Due-date restrictions, exact decimal allocation, charge safeguards and principal reconciliation remain with the existing repayment services.
- Batch validation retains both account IDs for each transaction. Posting compares that evidence and rechecks account eligibility; missing links, inactive/incompatible accounts or changed balances block posting. Old unposted batch previews must be revalidated. Account mappings are omitted from batch entry presentation. Product names remain visible alongside loans and allocations.

## Integrity and scope

Migration `V77__loan_product_account_links.sql` adds nullable paired product links and immutable loan bindings, with tenant/account foreign keys and deferred confirmation against the committed disbursement. The 20 October 2026 selection change in V83 removes the fixed principal/interest classifications from binding, disbursement, charged-receipt and top-up validation. Active posting/control eligibility, exact frozen account IDs, source/scope checks, balanced journals and independent linked corrections remain enforced. Top-up interest follows the source loan's frozen account. Existing rows are not backfilled or rewritten, and historical receipt corrections keep their original accounts. Mapping configuration and explicit linking emit accounting audit events.

The existing transaction, deterministic loan locking, idempotency, receipt envelope, independent linked reversal and batch worker lease controls remain. Top-up due-interest settlement uses the source loan's retained interest account; the replacement loan retains its own product accounts. No extra journal, approval step, role claim assignment, export format, pricing change or background worker is introduced.

This increment covers the existing directly recorded loan accounting and receipt-batch paths. It does not migrate legacy borrower-workflow loans into that accounting path, move historical balances, implement interest accrual, or complete reconciliation or the accounting/report-builder backlog. Income recognition and the institution's selected accounts remain institution decisions.
